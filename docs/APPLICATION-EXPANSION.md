# Application-question expansion — 27 October 2026

Added **48 original application cards**, eight per subject/paper, across all 24 topics. The deck now has **689 cards, including 91 application cards**. All 641 earlier cards and IDs are unchanged.

## Access and review batches

Before changing cards, all 72 bundled June 2023–2025 PDFs were checked against AQA’s catalogue hashes/sizes. All 1,915 pages extracted and rendered successfully. No missing/unreadable files or publisher omission notices were detected. The full inventory remains in [PAST-PAPERS.md](PAST-PAPERS.md).

The content review for this update uses the six June 2025 Higher papers and their six matching mark schemes. Biology, Chemistry and Physics were processed as separate four-file batches. Each new card records the precise source question and PDF pages. The other 60 documents were structurally verified and indexed; they were not given a fresh semantic review for this update. [Machine-readable batch and file status](application-expansion.json).

These are independently written practice questions, not transcriptions of AQA questions or official mark schemes. Contexts/numbers and the scope of prompts have been changed. Diagram/table-dependent tasks have been made self-contained. Longer evaluations are narrowed to a small explicit task; short card answers are not claimed to answer the original six-mark question in full. Answers use linked marking points and units rather than promising marks.

## Scope and tier checks

Each card maps to the supplied Combined Science 8464 specification snapshot. A source being a Higher paper does not make every concept Higher-only. Inverse-square light intensity, glucagon control, weak-acid ionisation, bond-energy calculations, equilibrium changes, tangent reaction rates, bioleaching, wavefront explanations and motor rotation are labelled Higher. Foundation cards cover shared content. The unfamiliar C-peptide relationship is supplied in the question. Source context does not add separate-science content.

Biology: cell adaptations, fair comparisons, plant tissue, microscopy, vaccine trials, inheritance, contraception, sampling, crop competition, biodiversity, fossil evidence and glucose control. Chemistry: electrolysis, controlled experiments, ions, intermolecular forces, acid strength, energy, open-system mass, hydrocarbon evidence, pollution, reaction rates and metal recovery. Physics: heat-loss errors, circuit corrections, ratings, radiation decisions, charging power, energy storage, forces, waves and uncertainty.
