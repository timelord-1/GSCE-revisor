## Trilogy Revision 1.2.0

Offline AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy revision with a complete personal study library.

- **Browse every flashcard:** all 641 built-in cards, including application and Higher questions, with subject/paper/topic/type/tier filters and searchable answers.
- **Edit and make cards:** save personal edits, restore originals, create your own core/application questions, and delete personal cards. Edits preserve schedules and are labelled as personal content.
- **Custom study plans:** choose cards or topics across subjects, study days, start/target dates, and daily new-card/review limits. Practice remains available on other days or after limits.
- **Application-only mode:** Settings offers Core only, All questions, or Application only; applies to reviews, practice and study search.
- Backups now include personal cards, edits, plans and modes. Existing progress, custom decks and legacy backups remain compatible.
- The built-in AQA library and its 25-PDF analysis record remain unchanged.

### Windows download

Run `Trilogy-Revision-1.2.0-Windows-x64.exe` on Windows 10 or later (x64). Portable, unsigned executable; no Node.js installation required. `SHA256SUMS.txt` records the checksum.

### Study details

Each rating counts as a review, including repeated ratings. Plan counters include today's ratings of its cards in any deck; the global new-card cap still applies. Target dates are goals, not expiry dates. Editing a card or deleting a deck/plan keeps card progress. Deleting a personal card removes its progress/history and removes empty selections after confirmation. Resetting progress keeps personal content and plans.

Personal cards and edits are labelled and have not been checked against the AQA specification. The 641 original cards still target Combined Science 8464, with all 225 subject-content sections and 21 practical activities mapped. References and original card IDs are retained. The source Biology paper's missing copyright photograph is documented in `docs/PDF-ANALYSIS.md`.
